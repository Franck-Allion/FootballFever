# Retrospective: Epic 13 - Squad Management (Gestion d'Effectif)

**Date:** 2026-05-08
**Project:** FootballFever
**Status:** Completed
**Participants:** Fal (Project Lead), Amelia (Developer), Alice (Product Owner), Charlie (Senior Dev), Dana (QA Engineer)

## 🎯 Epic Summary
Epic 13 transitioned the game from static mockups to a functional squad management system. It included roster generation, dynamic rating calculations based on position-specific weights, and a full drag-and-drop lineup editor.

### Delivery Metrics
- **Completed Stories:** 3/3 (100%)
- **Velocity:** High (all stories delivered with patches)
- **Quality:** 153/153 tests passing. 95%+ coverage maintained on domain logic.

## ✅ Successes & Strengths
- **UI/UX Excellence:** The drag-and-drop interface works smoothly on both PC and Mobile, respecting the "Obsidian Athletics" design theme.
- **Architectural Integrity:** Successfully implemented a strict separation between Persistence (IDs in TeamSchema) and Runtime (Hydrated Objects in Store/Services), following "AAA" standards.
- **Dynamic Feedback:** Live rating recomputation and position-eligibility badges (Green/Orange/Red) provide immediate tactical feedback.

## ⚠️ Challenges & Growth Areas
- **Mobile/PC Parity Hurdles:** Significant time was spent debugging `@dnd-kit` touch events and ghost rendering jumpiness on mobile.
- **Localization Invariants:** Several stories were submitted with hardcoded French strings, violating GEMINI.md rules and requiring a significant patching phase.
- **Complexity Management:** Mixing business logic (Rating formulas) and complex UI (DnD) in the same stories led to slower iterations and more bugs (ReferenceErrors).

## 💡 Key Insights
- **Game Feel matters:** The lack of audio feedback was identified as a missing piece for the "Tactician" experience.
- **Early Standardization:** Fixing the TeamSchema early saved the project from future database migration headaches.
